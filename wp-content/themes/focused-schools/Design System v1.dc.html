Brand-role rules (binding)

  1. Maximum two ground colors per page: white/paper plus teal.
  2. Coral is the only primary-button fill site-wide. One primary action per section.
  3. Service accents (cerulean / coral / lime) label a lane; they never restyle the component.
  4. Lime and gold never set type, never on a link, never on a button label.
  5. Raspberry appears only on Podcast and Blog surfaces.
  6. Body type is teal on light, white at 88% on teal. No gray on teal.
  7. No gradients, no tints of brand colors behind text below 4.5:1.

Motion tokens

  • --fs-dur-fast 160ms — color/opacity hovers
  • --fs-dur-base 240ms — transform hovers, accordion rows
  • --fs-dur-slow 520ms — one-time entrances
  • --fs-ease cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)

  • prefers-reduced-motion: all entrances and rotation disabled, hovers keep color only

Layout rules

Three container widths cover every homepage section. Internal pages must not introduce a fourth.

Full-bleed100vw · hero media, teal bands, image strip
Wide containermax 1440px · header, footer, image strip inner, carousels
Standard containermax 1200px · default for all content sections
Text measuremax 720px · intros, story body, legal
Grid
12 columns, 24px column gap desktop / 20px tablet. Cards snap to 4-col (thirds), 6-col (halves), or 3-col (quarters). No 5- or 7-column spans.
Gutters
48px ≥1280 · 32px 768–1279 · 20px <768. Gutters are page padding and are never overridden per section.
Alignment
Everything left-aligns to the container edge. Centered text is reserved for the teal Impact Stories heading and the footer tagline. No justified text, no centered body paragraphs.
Splits
Content/Image split is 6+6 at ≥1280, 7+5 when the copy column carries a list. 48–64px column gap. Media may bleed 48px past the container on the outer side only.
Vertical rhythm
Light section 120px top/bottom; teal or full-bleed section 160px; adjacent light sections of the same ground collapse to a single 120px seam.
Section heading block
Eyebrow → H2 → lead paragraph → optional button, gaps 12 / 20 / 28. Block max width 26ch for the H2, 62ch for the lead.

Responsive rules

Four breakpoints, mobile-first. Type scales at two steps only — no fluid clamp per element beyond the H1/H2 pair.

Desktop ≥1280

  • 1200px container, 48px gutter
  • H1 64 · H2 40 · body 17
  • Cards 3-up, splits 6+6
  • Section padding 120 / 160 teal

Laptop 1024–1279

  • Container fluid, 32px gutter
  • H1 52 · H2 36
  • Cards 3-up, splits 6+6 with 40px gap
  • Section padding 104 / 128

Tablet 768–1023

  • 32px gutter, 12-col becomes 8-col
  • H1 44 · H2 32 · body 17
  • Cards 2-up; stats stay 3-up; splits stack
  • Hamburger nav begins at 1023
  • Section padding 96

Mobile <768

  • 20px gutter, single column
  • H1 34 · H2 28 · H3 21 · body 16
  • Hero media 4:5, overlay card full-width with 20px side margins, −40px overlap
  • Cards 1-up, 20px gaps; carousels swipe, dots only
  • Buttons 44px min height, full-width when alone
  • Section padding 64 / 80 teal
  • Team/story grids paginate at 9 with a Load more secondary button
Deliverable F

Ambiguities needing human judgment

Each item below is a value I inferred from the mockup rather than read from a source of truth. Confirm or correct before page design starts.

1 · Exact container width

I read ~1200px content inside a ~1440px wide band from the mockup. If the built homepage uses a different max-width, that number wins and I will restate the grid.

2 · Service accent vs. homepage accordion

The copy assigns cerulean / coral / lime to the three lanes, but the homepage "How we help" list shows four items (incl. Technical Assistance) with coral only. Decide: does the homepage list adopt lane accents, or does Services own them alone?

3 · Lime as an accent on light grounds

Lime is a fill-only color and fails contrast as a rule on white at 2px. I have used it for active rules and on teal only. Confirm that lime never appears as a hairline on white.

4 · Partner display: chips vs. logos

I specced text chips because district logos vary wildly and usage rights are unclear. If the client has permission and files, the strip becomes a 5-up logo grid at 40px cap height.

5 · Team photography

4:5 portraits assume consistent headshots exist. If they do not, the fallback is a teal tile with initials in white 900 — needs approval, and the two cannot mix within one grid.

6 · Legacy impact stories

Legacy stories may lack a cover image, state tag, or excerpt. My fallback is the teal watermark tile + auto-excerpt. Confirm whether legacy stories get backfilled instead.

7 · Podcast source of truth

Buzzsprout is preserved, and the video card is designed for a later YouTube playlist feed. Confirm whether episode metadata comes from Buzzsprout RSS or YouTube, since the card shows duration.

8 · Error/validation color

The palette has no red and the guide says use neutral gray for negatives. I specced gray. Confirm that gray-only validation is acceptable to the client on the contact form.

9 · Login / client portal

The header carries a Login button. Is the destination in scope for this refresh, or does it stay as-is?

10 · Cycle of Excellence reuse

The rotating Cycle is a homepage signature. May the About and Services pages reuse it, or should internal pages reference the Framework statically?

Deliverable G

Developer handoff notes

Theme structure

  • Tokens live in one _tokens.css on :root; no hex in templates or ACF fields.
  • Each component above maps to one reusable block/pattern (native block or ACF flexible-content layout), not a bespoke page template.
  • Section wrapper takes two controls only: ground (white / paper / teal) and padding (default / tight). Nothing else is editor-configurable.
  • Page templates are compositions of these blocks; a new page should need zero new CSS.

Dynamic content

  • Team — CPT + repeatable card grid; arbitrary count, no manual duplication; grid reflows 3/2/1.
  • Services — one service template, lane accent stored as a taxonomy/field value mapping to a token.
  • Impact Stories — archive query populates the card; legacy + CPT share one card partial with documented fallbacks.
  • Podcast — static thumbnail + lazy iframe on click; Buzzsprout embed preserved; playlist feed cached server-side.
  • Contact — existing form plugin and business logic untouched; restyled via the form tokens only.

Performance & a11y

  • DM Sans self-hosted, woff2, weights 400/500/700/900 + 400/700 italic; font-display: swap; subset latin.
  • Hero image eager + fetchpriority=high; all other media lazy with width/height to prevent CLS.
  • Focus visible on every interactive element: 3px cerulean ring, 2px offset — never removed.
  • Accordion and carousel are real buttons with aria-expanded / aria-roledescription; carousel is keyboard-navigable.
  • Honor prefers-reduced-motion: kill entrances and the Cycle rotation.
  • Contrast verified: teal/white 8.4:1, gray/white 6.9:1, white/teal 8.4:1, coral button white label 3.6:1 — so button labels stay ≥16px/700 only.

What happens next

  1. Designer + Briggs review this document and resolve the ten ambiguities.
  2. I lock v1 with their corrections (no page design before that gate).
  3. Page design proceeds in brief order: Services → About → Team → Impact Stories landing → Impact Story single → Podcast → Contact.
  4. Each page ships with section hierarchy, desktop + mobile mockups, component mapping, type/spacing specs, image ratios, states, long-content handling, and dev notes.
